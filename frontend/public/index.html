How to play

  1. One player taps Create Room and shares the code. Everyone else taps Join Room and enters it. 2 to 8 players.
  2. In the lobby, scan every player: hold a phone on them while they turn slowly in one full circle.
  3. Tap Launch. After the countdown, aim the crosshair at a player and tap anywhere to fire.
  4. Everyone has 100 HP. A body shot does 20, a headshot 50. Last player standing wins.
